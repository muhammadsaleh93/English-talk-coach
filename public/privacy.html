Privacy Policy

App: English Talk Coach
Developer: Muhammad Saleh
Last updated: 3 November 2026

What the app does

English Talk Coach helps you practise English conversation with an AI coach.

Information we process

  • Chat messages: The text you type or speak in a lesson is sent to our server and to Google's Gemini AI service to create the coach's reply. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number, and we do not store your messages on our server.
  • Voice input: When you tap the microphone, your device's speech recognition (provided by Google on Android) turns your voice into text. Only the text is sent to the coach. The app does not record or keep audio.
  • Progress and settings: Your name, settings, progress, learned words and recent conversations are saved only on your own device. You can delete them any time in Profile → "Reset my progress", or by clearing the app's data. "Save backup" creates a file on your phone; it is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Security logs: To stop abuse, our server counts requests per IP address for a short time (minutes). We do not store IP addresses with your messages.

Third-party services

  • Google Gemini API — creates AI replies. See Google Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — hosts the app and server. See Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Advertising and selling data

The app has no ads, and we do not sell or share your personal data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

AI content

AI replies may sometimes be inaccurate. You can report any offensive or wrong reply with the "⚑ Report" button under each coach message.
